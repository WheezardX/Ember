## 2. Vegetation tiers

| Tier | Distance from camera | Representation | Wind | Shadows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Near** | 0 – ~0.8 km | Full 3D trees (B3 v2 growth-form meshes; later modular, §3) | full sway + flutter | live (VSM invalidated each frame) |
| **Mid** | ~0.8 – 6 km | Octahedral impostors: one camera-facing card per tree sampling a per-species atlas baked from the near meshes | none (WPO off) | cached |
| **Far** | 6 km + | No instances: canopy colour in the terrain albedo (have) + canopy height (CHM x cover) raised into far terrain LODs so ridgelines carry a forest silhouette | — | terrain's |

* Tier edges cross-fade by per-instance dither over ~100 m so no ring is visible.
* Placement is identical in every tier (the conformant scatter); only the representation changes.
* Impostor atlases are per species and variant, not per AOI — nothing baked per incident (D3).
* Budget levers, in order: near radius, mid density thinning (keep the largest crowns), impostor
  resolution.

## 3. Tree variation (Brad, HCP2 round 1: "Lego them together")

Today: 4 whole-tree variants per species (`treegen.VARIANTS`), picked by a position hash, plus
per-instance height, crown width, yaw, tint and lean.

Long term, for the near tier: **modular trees**. A tree = trunk + 2–3 crown bands (lower / middle /
top), each band with 4–5 variants generated by the same growth-form code, assembled per instance
with an independent random rotation of each band about the trunk. 5 x 5 x 5 = 125 combinations per
species from 15 small meshes, before rotation, scale and tint - repetition becomes invisible.
Each section is an ordinary instance, so instancing still works; the cost is 3–4 instances per
near tree (~0.5–0.8 M instances at the near radius, fine for Nanite). Mid/far tiers stay whole-
tree impostors, where repetition cannot be seen. UE 5.7+ ships experimental Nanite Assemblies
aimed at exactly this; evaluate what 5.8 exposes before building sections as plain instances.

## 4. World context (HCP1: "Google-Maps-like LOD")

* The terrain quadtree extends past the incident AOI: AOI tiles at 10 m (LiDAR), then a surround
  ring at 30 m (3DEP 1 arc-second) and 90 m beyond, each ring its own Terrain region with the same
  manifest/tile format, stitched by skirts at ring edges (fixes the region-edge "diorama cliff").
* Land cover in the surround: LANDFIRE FBFM40/EVT at 30 m through the same look; no trees
  instanced outside the AOI (far tier canopy only).
* Continuous zoom: the streaming manager already selects LOD by distance; the surround adds
  coarser root tiles. Past ~100 km a cartographic basemap (Epic 9 CDN tiles) takes over.
* Streaming: tile compose/mesh on worker threads (HCP1 finding 3) before any of this ships.
